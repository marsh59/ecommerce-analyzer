Connect your MySQL database or upload a CSV — then explore sales trends, product insights, and customer behaviour through interactive dashboards.

About this dataset: This platform is built around a UK-based registered non-store online retail dataset covering transactions from December 2010 to December 2011. The dataset contains over 500,000 transactions made by approximately 4,300 unique customers across 38 countries. Each record includes invoice number, product description, quantity, price, date, and customer ID. After uploading, the data is automatically cleaned — guest checkouts and cancelled orders are removed — giving you a reliable foundation for analysis. You will then be able to explore monthly revenue trends, top-performing products, most valuable customers, a 6-month sales forecast, and a full RFM customer segmentation — all from a single dashboard.
