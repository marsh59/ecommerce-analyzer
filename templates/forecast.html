What is Sales Forecasting?

Sales forecasting is the process of predicting future revenue based on patterns found in historical sales data. Instead of guessing, it uses a mathematical model to study how sales have behaved in the past — whether they are growing, declining, or following a seasonal pattern — and then projects that behaviour forward into the future. This forecast is powered by Holt-Winters Exponential Smoothing, a reliable and widely-used statistical method for time-series forecasting. It works by giving more weight to recent sales data than older data, so the model is always more influenced by what happened recently rather than a long time ago. The model was trained on monthly sales figures and learned the underlying trend to predict what the next 6 months of sales are likely to look like. Forecasting helps businesses plan inventory, set revenue targets, and make smarter decisions before problems arise.
