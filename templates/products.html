Top Products by Revenue

A horizontal bar chart displays categories along the vertical axis and their values along the horizontal axis. This layout is ideal when category names are long (like product descriptions), as it gives each label enough space to be fully readable. Each bar represents total revenue earned by that product.

Out of {{ total_products }} unique products in the catalogue, the top revenue generator is {{ top_product_name }}, which earned £{{ best_product_rev }} in total. {{ second_product }} is the second highest earning product. The chart reveals that revenue is not evenly spread — a small number of products contribute a disproportionately large share of total sales. This is a classic example of the Pareto Principle (80/20 rule), where a handful of items drive the majority of revenue. The business should ensure these top products are always well-stocked and prominently promoted.

Revenue by Category

A donut chart is a circular chart divided into slices, where each slice represents a category's proportion of the total. The hollow centre makes it easier to read the overall shape compared to a full pie chart. Here, each slice represents a product category derived from the first word of the product description.
