The count of unique invoice numbers. Each invoice represents one complete customer order, which may contain multiple products across several rows.
Avg Order Value
£{{ avg_order_value }}
Total Revenue divided by Total Orders. This tells you how much money a customer spends on average per single order. A higher value means customers are buying more per visit.
